Know this for the live: it prints on special thermal paper (not regular copy paper) — that's how it runs with no ink. Thermal prints can fade over time in heat/sunlight, so it's best for everyday/temporary docs. The 203 DPI is great for text; for sharper photos, point them to the 300 DPI MT800.
