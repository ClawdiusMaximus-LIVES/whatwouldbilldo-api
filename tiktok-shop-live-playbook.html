TikTok Shop LIVE Playbook

Keep on a 2nd screen while you sell
The rule: Shop lives are ranked on engagement × sales. Your master metric is GPM (revenue per 1,000 views). High GPM → TikTok sends more shoppers → more sales → more impressions. Everything here pushes GPM up.

① Pre-Live Warm-Up

  1. Post 1–3 short videos tagged "LIVE NOW" 0–10 min before — your feed is the #1 funnel in.
  2. Stable connection, good lighting, clear audio (low-quality streams get demoted).
  3. Products staged in reach, hero deal ready to pin first.
  4. Go live at your same peak hour daily (check LIVE analytics for when your audience is on).

② First 5 Minutes

  1. Pin a product immediately — never an empty cart.
  2. Greet joiners by name, set the hook: "here's what we're doing today."
  3. State the live-only deal up front to hold viewers.
  4. Get a first comment fast: "type 1 if you can hear me."

③ The 90-Second Loop (repeat all stream)

RE-HOOK: "Just joined? We're doing [deal] on the [product] — only [#] left."
DEMO: Show it working — [the waterproof test / the sound / the feature]. Keep it on screen.
CTA: "Tap the orange cart, bottom left, grab it before the price goes back up."
ENGAGE: Call out a new name + ask a question: "type YES if you want the bundle."

Never be mid-story with no product on screen — TikTok sends shopper batches in waves; convert them the second they arrive.

④ Timed Deal Schedule

ClockMove
:00Pin hero product, announce live-only price
:15Flash deal — "next 5 min only," countdown
:30New product + restock the last one
:45Giveaway / bundle for anyone who ordered
:60Recap best deal, re-pin, repeat cycle

Time deals to viewer spikes — a burst of orders triggers a bigger push.

⑥ Never Do (Reach Killers)

  • Mention prices/deals off-platform or push other apps.
  • Exaggerated claims ("waterproof forever," health cures) — violations cap reach.
  • Empty or wrong pinned product during a traffic spike.
  • Dead air / stepping away — watch time tanks.
  • Ignore new joiners — they leave, engagement drops.

⑦ Go-Live Checklist

  • Warm-up videos posted
  • Lighting + audio checked
  • Products staged & priced
  • Hero deal ready to pin
  • Live-only coupon set
  • Countdown/deal times noted
  • Analytics tab open (2nd screen)
  • Plan to stay live 2+ hours
