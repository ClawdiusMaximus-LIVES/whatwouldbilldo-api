ANIMAT3D Jabberin' Jack

Talking Animated Pumpkin · Live Sell Sheet
What it is: A talking animated Halloween pumpkin with a built-in video projector & speaker inside the shell — its face comes alive singing, talking, and joking. Plug'n play, no carving, reusable every year.

Top Selling Points (say these live)

  • 70–80 minutes of singing, talking & joking animations — it doesn't repeat fast
  • 3 personalities — Spooky, Traditional & Funny — kid-friendly or scary, their choice
  • Projector face brings the pumpkin to life — animated eyes & mouth, looks real
  • Motion-sensor models auto-activate — greets trick-or-treaters as they walk up
  • Plug'n play — zero setup, no carving, no mess; store it and reuse every year
  • Built-in speaker — loud enough to fill the porch
  • The hit of the neighborhood — a talking porch centerpiece, not just a decoration

Content & Modes

  • 70+ min content (standard) · 80 min (XL motion-sensor)
  • 3 characters: Spooky · Traditional · Funny
  • Silent facial expressions between clips
  • Still mode — pause on a face
  • Motion-activated (XL model)

How to Control It

  • Power: plug in the USB cable (included)
  • Volume +/−: two of the three buttons
  • Skip: 3rd button advances to next animation
  • Still mode: pause the current animation
  • Motion sensor (XL): auto lights up & talks
