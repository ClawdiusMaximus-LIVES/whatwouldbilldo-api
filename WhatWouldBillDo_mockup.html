Design Notes
🎨 Palette

Parchment #F5EDD6 — main background, feels like the Big Book's actual pages.
Amber #C8860A — single accent color, candle warmth.
Ink #2C1810 — deep brown, not black — feels aged and warm.

#F5EDD6 bg #C8860A accent #2C1810 text #C4A96A border
✍️ Typography

EB Garamond for Bill's voice, headings, and onboarding — feels like a letter, not an app.
Inter for all UI chrome, buttons, labels — clean contrast with the serif.
Monospace for citations, metadata, dates — credibility without clutter.

💬 Bill's Bubble

Corner radius: 3pt top-left, 16pt elsewhere — asymmetric, like a letter arriving from one direction. Subtle off-white (#FFFDF8) vs parchment background — just enough contrast to feel like a card.

🕯️ The Candle

CSS-only animated candle flame. No image dependency. Flicker animation gives it life without being distracting. Used on onboarding, paywall, and as the app icon concept.

💰 Paywall Design

Dark leather background contrasts with the parchment app — signals "you're about to unlock something." Monthly highlighted as Popular. Yearly shows the $5/mo equivalent. Amber CTA on dark = maximum contrast.

🚨 Crisis Screen

Deep crimson/charcoal background — distinct from everything else in the app. No Bill persona here. First resource card (988) highlighted in red. "Bill can wait" framing removes any guilt about switching away from the AI.
