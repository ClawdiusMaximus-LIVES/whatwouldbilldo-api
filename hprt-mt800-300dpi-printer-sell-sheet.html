Know this for the live: the MT800 (300 DPI) uses a thermal ribbon inside (≈45 sheets each) — still no messy ink, but the ribbon is a consumable, unlike the direct-thermal 203 DPI MT610 which uses only special paper. Glossy-surface paper gives the sharpest results. Confirm ribbon/paper included in your box.
