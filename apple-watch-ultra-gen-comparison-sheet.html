Which One Is Right For You?

Ultra (Gen 1)

  • Best value into the rugged Ultra line.
  • All the core adventure features.
  • Great if 36 hrs & LTE is plenty.
  • Ideal budget pick on resale.

Ultra 2 (Gen 2)

  • Brighter 3,000-nit display.
  • Adds Double Tap gesture.
  • Faster S9 chip, on-device Siri.
  • Sweet spot of price vs features.

Ultra 3 (Gen 3)

  • 5G + built-in satellite SOS/messages.
  • Longest battery — up to 42 hrs.
  • Bigger, wider-angle display.
  • New health: hypertension & Sleep Score.
  • Fastest charging + newest S10 chip.
